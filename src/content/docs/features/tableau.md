---
title: Tableau
description: The real-time board of the day's orders, by status.
---

The Tableau is the main working screen during service. It shows the active Store's orders of the current day in three columns: **Pending**, **In progress** and **Completed**.

- **Real time**: the board updates as orders are created and changed. It restarts at midnight and when the active Store changes.
- **Order**: Pending and In progress are sorted by scheduled time, earliest first. Completed is sorted by completion time, latest first. Editing a ticket does not move it.
- **Cancelled tickets** are not shown.
- **Layout**: on large screens each column scrolls on its own; on tablets the columns scroll horizontally; on phones one column is shown at a time through tabs.

Each ticket is a compact card that expands in place. See [Tickets](/pizzahub-docs/features/tickets/) for the actions and [Print preview](/pizzahub-docs/features/print-preview/) for the receipt.
