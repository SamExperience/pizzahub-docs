The Dashboard has a section sidebar. On small screens it becomes a selector above the content.

## Order history

- Choose a day (default today; future days are not allowed). The page lists the Store's orders created on that day, newest first, with the creation time, customer name, order type, status and total.
- Cancelled orders are included, greyed out.
- Click a row to expand its read-only detail: item snapshots, customer, scheduled and completion times and total.
- The orders are read once when the page opens or the day changes. The history is not real time.

## Subscription

The **Subscription** section shows the status of the Company's demo plan and a **Subscribe** button. See [Subscription](/pizzahub-docs/subscription/) for details.
