---
title: Onboarding
description: Company and Store setup for the first User of a pizzeria.
---

The first time a User signs up, they create their Company and first Store.

- A form split into a **Company** section and a **Store** section, with required-field validation.
- The first User is created with the `admin` role and linked to the Company and the Store through their Firebase UID.
- Each Company has one User, the admin, created here. Later changes to the Company, the Store or the role are not allowed from the app.

After onboarding, the User continues to the [Menu](/pizzahub-docs/features/menu/) until it is ready, see [Store selection](/pizzahub-docs/features/store-selection/).
