---
title: Print preview
description: Receipt preview formatted for a 58 mm thermal printer.
---

Every ticket can be previewed as a receipt, formatted as plain text for a 58 mm thermal printer (32 characters per line). It shows the Store, the date and time, the order type, the customer (phone and address for delivery), the items with their options and customizations, right-aligned prices and the total.

- Open it with the printer icon on a ticket card in the [Tableau](/pizzahub-docs/features/tableau/).
- **Print on save** in the ticket sidebar opens the preview after a new ticket is saved. It is on by default and remembered on each device. Edits never open it automatically.

:::note
No printer is connected yet. **Print** closes the preview, the same as **Close**; it does not send anything to a printer.
:::
